## Not yet confirmed for participant use

- FinReason registration URL and registration close
- Final platform architecture and account-to-team mapping
- Per-task data releases, licenses, manifests, and checksums
- Official scorer versions, primary metrics, and tie-breaks
- Submission limits, external-resource policy, and invalid-submission policy
- Public/private leaderboard split and final rerun procedure
- Source-code, notebook, container, or network-access requirements
- Six-page report route, review status, and presentation selection
- Award eligibility, funding, certificate wording, and registration support

## Public-wording rule

The website may repeat confirmed conference context. Everything else remains
marked `pre-launch`, `provisional`, or `coming soon` until the corresponding
release evidence exists. If IEEE's overview and the final FinReason
implementation differ, organizers must either update the implementation or ask
IEEE to correct the overview before registration opens.
